# Script 02 — Your Supporters Are Your Threat

**Platform:** TikTok / YouTube Shorts
**Angle:** Dark psychology / power dynamics / Machiavelli
**Runtime:** ~80 seconds spoken
**Tone:** Cold, confident, slightly threatening

---

## HOOK (0–3s)
**[TEXT ON SCREEN — bold black on white, center]:**
"Your biggest threat isn't your enemy. It's your cheerleader."

*Silence. 2-beat hold. 3D chess board or pieces, white background.*

---

## SCENE 1 — Wrong thing first (3–12s)
**[VO]:**
"You've been taught to watch your enemies.

The people who criticize you. The ones who don't believe in you.
The ones who say it won't work."

**[TEXT]:** "Watch your enemies."

---

## SCENE 2 — Pivot hard (12–22s)
**[VO]:**
"Wrong people to watch.

The people studying you most carefully right now
are the ones smiling at you."

**[TEXT — large, slow fade in]:** "The ones smiling at you."

**[3D VISUAL]:** Chess king piece toppling over in slow motion, white background

---

## SCENE 3 — Unanswerable question (22–35s)
**[VO]:**
"Think about the people closest to you.

When you announced something new —
a plan, a goal, a move they didn't expect —
which ones got a little quieter?

Not mean. Not obvious. Just... quieter.

Why?"

**[TEXT]:** "Just. Quieter."

**[SILENCE — 3 full seconds. White screen. No text. No music.]*

---

## SCENE 4 — Dark truth (35–62s)
**[VO]:**
"Machiavelli wrote about this five hundred years ago.

He said the most dangerous people to a rising power
aren't the open enemies.

They're the ones who've built their identity around being close to you —
while you were still small.

Here's why:

Your enemy wants you gone. That's manageable. You know what they want.

But your supporter?
They've built a relationship on a particular version of you.
One that needs them. One that asks for advice.
One that hasn't outgrown them yet.

The moment you stop needing them — the dynamic inverts.

Now they're not supporting you.
They're competing with who you're becoming.

And they'll do it with a smile.
Because they don't even know it's happening."

**[TEXT — appears in fragments, builds tension]:**
"A version of you that needs them."
*→ pause →*
"Who you're becoming."

**[3D VISUAL]:** Two chess pieces — one slowly eclipsing the other. White bg. Cold light.

**[MUSIC: ambient tension, builds slowly, peaks here, -15db under VO]*

---

## SCENE 5 — Turn it back (62–80s)
**[VO]:**
"The question isn't who believes in you.

The question is — who benefits from you staying exactly where you are?

Those people will call it love.
Call it concern.
Call it 'being realistic.'

Learn to hear what they're actually saying."

**[FINAL TEXT — black, large, white bg, alone on screen]:**
"Who needs you to stay small?"

**[HOLD 3s. Cut to black.]*

---

## COMMENT HOOK
"Name one person in your life who got weird when you started winning."

---

## VISUAL NOTES
- White background — clean, clinical, cold
- 3D objects: chess pieces (king + queen), mirror, eye — white bg
- Chess piece slow topple = THE most powerful visual moment (SCENE 2)
- Text: Typewriter effect or word-by-word Fade In (CapCut)
- Font: bold black — heavy weight, commanding
- Music: starts at SCENE 3, tension builds through SCENE 4, cuts at FINAL TEXT
- Final cut: hard cut to black (not white) — creates contrast, ends on unease
- Captions: auto-generate CapCut, bottom 1/3 screen, minimal clean font
