# Script 02 — Your Supporters Are Your Threat

**Platform:** TikTok / YouTube Shorts
**Angle:** Dark psychology / power dynamics
**Runtime:** ~80 seconds spoken
**Tone:** Cold, confident, a little unsettling

---

## HOOK (0–3s)
**[TEXT ON SCREEN]:** "Your biggest threat isn't your enemy. It's your cheerleader."

*Hold 2 beats.*

---

## OPENING STATEMENT — say the wrong thing first
**[VO]:**
"You've been taught to watch your enemies.

The people who criticize you. The ones who don't believe in you. The ones who say it won't work."

---

## THE PIVOT — flip it
**[VO]:**
"Wrong people to watch.

The people studying you most carefully right now are the ones smiling at you."

---

## THE UNANSWERABLE QUESTION
**[VO]:**
"Think about the people closest to you.

When you announced something new — a plan, a goal, a move they didn't expect — which ones got a little quieter?

Not mean. Not obvious. Just... quieter.

Why?"

---

## THE DARK TRUTH
**[VO]:**
"Machiavelli wrote about this five hundred years ago.

He said the most dangerous people to a rising power aren't the open enemies. They're the ones who've built their identity around being *close to you* — while you were still small.

Here's why:

Your enemy wants you gone. That's manageable. You know what they want.

But your supporter? They've built a relationship on a particular version of you. One that needs them. One that asks for advice. One that hasn't outgrown them yet.

The moment you stop needing them — the dynamic inverts.

Now they're not supporting you. They're competing with who you're becoming.

And they'll do it with a smile. Because they don't even know it's happening."

---

## TURN IT BACK PERSONALLY
**[VO]:**
"The question isn't who believes in you.

The question is: who benefits from you staying exactly where you are?

Those people will call it love.
Call it concern.
Call it 'being realistic.'

Learn to hear what they're actually saying."

---

## CLOSE / VISUAL
**[TEXT ON SCREEN]:** "Watch who celebrates your wins — and watch who goes quiet."

**[HOOK FOR COMMENTS]:** "Name one person in your life who got weird when you started winning."

---

## VISUAL NOTES
- Surveillance / close-up face imagery — no identity shown
- Cut to black at "the moment you stop needing them"
- Slow zoom on text overlays
- Music: building tension, no resolution — leave it uncomfortable
- Color grade: cold — desaturated blues/greens
