# Script 03 — Morality Is a Leash

**Platform:** TikTok / YouTube Shorts
**Angle:** Dark philosophy / Nietzsche + Machiavelli
**Runtime:** ~80 seconds spoken
**Tone:** Cold, intellectually threatening, no apology

---

## HOOK (0–3s)
**[TEXT ON SCREEN — bold black on white, center]:**
"You weren't taught to be good. You were taught to be manageable."

*No music yet. Silence. 3D chain or lock object slow-rotating.*

---

## SCENE 1 — Say the wrong thing first (3–12s)
**[VO]:**
"Every moral lesson you were ever taught — be kind, share, don't be selfish, put others first —
was given to you by someone who benefited from you following it."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN]:** "Every. Single. One."

---

## SCENE 2 — The pivot (12–28s)
**[VO]:**
"Nietzsche called it slave morality.

The weak — the powerless — invented a morality that turned their weaknesses into virtues.
Couldn't compete? Humility became holy.
Couldn't accumulate? Poverty became noble.
Couldn't fight? Meekness became blessed.

The people writing the rules made the rules work for them.
And they called it righteousness."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN — word by word]:**
"Humility. Noble. Blessed."
*Each word hits like a verdict.*

**[3D VISUAL]:** Floating chess king piece, slow rotate, white background

---

## SCENE 3 — The unanswerable question (28–40s)
**[VO]:**
"Ask yourself:

Who told you that ambition was greedy?
That wanting more was selfish?
That success should feel like guilt?

And what did they have to gain from you believing that?"

**[SILENCE — 2 full seconds. No music. No text. White screen.]*

---

## SCENE 4 — The dark truth (40–65s)
**[VO]:**
"Machiavelli knew this.

Morality is a tool of power. Not a truth.

The people who control morality — control behavior.
Control behavior — control outcomes.
Control outcomes — control people.

And they don't need chains. They don't need force.
They just need you to believe that what's good for them is also right for you.

That's the leash.
Not physical. Psychological.
And you chose to put it on."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN — slow fade in one line at a time]:**
"Not a chain. A belief."

**[3D VISUAL]:** Slow-rotating glass or crystal brain on white, almost beautiful

---

## SCENE 5 — Turn it back personally (65–80s)
**[VO]:**
"This isn't about being immoral.

It's about knowing WHOSE morality you're living by.

And whether — if you actually had a choice —
you would choose the same rules."

**[FINAL TEXT ON SCREEN — black, large, alone]:**
"Whose rules are you following?"

**[HOLD 3 seconds. Fade to white.]*

---

## COMMENT HOOK
"What's one 'moral' rule you were taught that only benefited the person teaching it?"
