# Philosophy Video — Production Style Guide

## Visual System (White Minimal Style)
- **Background:** Pure white (#FFFFFF) or off-white (#F5F5F0) — NO gradients
- **Text:** Black bold serif or clean sans-serif — center-aligned
- **3D Objects:** Floating, slow-rotating on white — brain, chess piece, clock, eye, mirror, hand
- **Motion:** Text fades in word-by-word OR types on — never slides in
- **Color accent:** ONE color max per video — deep red, dark navy, or gold for 1 word
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920
- **Length:** 60–90 seconds

## Editing in CapCut
1. White background base layer (solid color, full screen)
2. 3D object layer — download from Mixkit/Pexels, slow speed (60–70%)
3. Text layer — use CapCut's "Typewriter" or "Fade In" animation
4. VO audio — record clean, no reverb
5. Background music — low ambient, -20db under voice
6. Cuts: hard cuts only — no wipes, no transitions
7. Captions: auto-generate, clean minimal font, bottom 1/3
