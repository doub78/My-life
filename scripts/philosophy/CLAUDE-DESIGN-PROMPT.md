# Claude Design Prompt — Philosophy Video Visual System

## USE THIS PROMPT IN CLAUDE DESIGN TO CREATE THUMBNAIL / TITLE CARDS

---

### PROMPT TO PASTE:

```
Create a set of 3 TikTok title cards for a dark philosophy video channel.

STYLE:
- Pure white background (#FFFFFF)
- Bold black typography — heavy weight, no decorative fonts
- ONE 3D object per card: floating, centered, slow-rotation implied (static render)
- Minimalist — no extra elements, no borders, no gradients
- Cold, clinical, slightly uncomfortable feeling
- Format: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920px)

CARD 1 — "Your Kindness Is Manipulation"
- 3D object: transparent glass hand, open palm, white background
- Main text: "Kind people are the most manipulative." — bold black, center, large
- Subtext: (none — let the statement breathe)
- Mood: still, unsettling, beautiful

CARD 2 — "Your Supporters Are Your Threat"
- 3D object: chess king piece, white marble texture, slightly tilted
- Main text: "Your biggest threat is smiling at you." — bold black, center
- Subtext: (none)
- Mood: power, cold, strategic

CARD 3 — "Morality Is a Leash"
- 3D object: glass brain, crystalline, catching light, floating
- Main text: "You weren't taught to be good. You were taught to be manageable." — bold black, center, slightly smaller to fit
- Subtext: (none)
- Mood: intellectual, threatening, beautiful

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Font style: Montserrat Black or similar heavy geometric sans-serif
- Size: fill 60-70% of card width
- Color: pure black on white — maximum contrast
- Letter-spacing: tight (-0.02em)
- No italics

OVERALL:
- These should feel like they belong in an art gallery AND on TikTok at the same time
- The 3D objects are rendered beautifully — not cartoonish
- White space is the design — don't fill it
- No background color other than white
- If you add any accent color, use: #1a1a1a (near-black) or #8B0000 (dark red) — one word only
```
